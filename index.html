About

Modelling how power systems get to zero carbon

I am a researcher working on sustainable energy transition, with a focus on power sector decarbonization: electricity sector modelling, national and regional decarbonization scenarios, integration of demand-side solutions into supply planning, and the socio-economic consequences of transition pathways. Much of my work is built and released as open-source models, so that national planners and researchers can reuse and challenge it.

I am currently based in Saudi Arabia as a postdoctoral researcher at the Interdisciplinary Research Center for Smart Mobility and Logistics (IRC-SML), King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals, where my work addresses decarbonization in the Kingdom and the wider MENA region. I remain a guest researcher with the SMARTS Center at the Asian Institute of Technology, Thailand, where I developed PyPSA-BD and PyPSA-TH — open-source national power system models for Bangladesh and Thailand.

My research appears in journals including Energy Strategy Reviews, Renewable Energy Focus, Energy for Sustainable Development, Energy Reports, Energy Conversion and Management: X and Sustainability Science, alongside IEEE conference proceedings and book chapters.
