Events

Selected events where I presented research, served as a resource person, or contributed to international working groups — most recent first.

Invited speaker · 5 November 2025 · Online

EDITS Webinar #11 — Integrating demand-side solutions for accelerated decarbonization

I spoke at the EDITS Webinar #11 on "Integrating Demand-side Solutions for Accelerated Decarbonization of the Power Supply System: Insights from Bangladesh and Thailand". The session was moderated by Jubair Sieed (RITE, Japan) and organised under the Energy Demand changes Induced by Technological and Social innovations (EDITS) project.

Presenter · 18–19 September 2025 · Bangkok, Thailand

International Workshop on Asian Perspectives in Integrated Assessment Modeling

Jointly organised by the Asian Institute of Technology and Yonsei University, South Korea. I contributed two presentations:

  • Energy sector modelling: a review of models and low-carbon technologies in South and Southeast Asia
  • Integrating demand-side solutions for accelerated decarbonization of the power supply system
Invited speaker · 11 September 2025 · Online

Energy Evaluation Asia Pacific Webinar Series #21

The series themed "Industrial Energy Transitions in South Asia: Policies, Impacts, and Future Pathways". My talk was "Industrial Energy Efficiency in Bangladesh: Assessing the Journey and Looking Ahead".

Presenter · 10–13 June 2025 · Nara, Japan

International Energy Workshop 2025

I presented "PyPSA-BD for strategic power sector planning in Bangladesh with gradual renewable energy penetration, end-use enhanced efficiency and demand-side flexibility". The results show that combining efficiency improvements and demand-side flexibility with renewable expansion reduces overreliance on large-scale renewable deployment, and lets Bangladesh move more efficiently toward a carbon-neutral power system.

Resource person · 17–19 March 2025 · AIT, Thailand

International Training Course on Energy Policy and Regulatory Framework

An international training course on "Energy, Policy, Act and Regulatory Framework Development" for officials from the Department of Energy, Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources, Bhutan. I delivered sessions on sustainable energy transitions, regulatory frameworks, stakeholder engagement, and the socio-economic dimensions of energy planning.

Winning team lead · 27–28 February 2025 · Bangkok, Thailand

ADB Youth Capacity Workshop — Developing Youth Solutions for Urban Climate Resilience

Our team developed Eco-Cooler, a low-cost nature-based cooling solution designed to help outdoor workers cope with extreme heat. The project took first place among all participating teams.

Participant · 2024 · IIASA, Laxenburg, Austria

EDITS 2024 Annual Meeting

The annual meeting of the EDITS network at IIASA, focused on global demand-side energy solutions and contributions toward IPCC AR7.

Oral presentation · 21–23 October 2024 · Pattaya City, Thailand

ICUE 2024 — Energy Transition and Net-Zero Climate Future

I presented "PyPSA-BD: an open-source model for planning a sustainable power sector for Bangladesh".
